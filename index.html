About Us

The Basis Phoenix Science Bowl Club is a welcoming space where students sharpen scientific thinking through friendly competition. Members collaborate, practice rapid problem-solving, and explore exciting questions across many branches of science while building confidence and teamwork.

Why Join

  • Strengthen core science and math skills
  • Build quick recall and collaboration
  • Compete in an encouraging team environment
